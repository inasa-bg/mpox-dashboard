Background

The first case of the current mpox outbreak had symptom onset on 13 June 2026 and was laboratory-confirmed on 23 June 2026. The Ministry of Public Health (MINSAP) officially declared the outbreak on 4 July 2026, and sequencing identified clade IIb on 5 July 2026. The response is coordinated by the incident management system (SGI-Mpox), activated on 1 July 2026, with support from the National Institute of Public Health (INASA) and WHO.

The outbreak first affected the Bissau health region (SAB), then Biombo (first confirmed case on 13 July 2026) and Bafatá (14 September 2026). This dashboard presents data from the case line list and the contact follow-up database, and is updated whenever INASA publishes new data.
